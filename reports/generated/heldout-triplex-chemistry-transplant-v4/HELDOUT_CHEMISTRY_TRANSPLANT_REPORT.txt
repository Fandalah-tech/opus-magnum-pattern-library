Chemistry transplant search
---------------------------
The new search does not scan arbitrary board coordinates. For every retained
mechanism it first replays the arm and track without molecules, records the
distinct cells where a grab instruction actually occurs, then anchors each
target reagent atom to those observed cells under all six rotations.

For each input placement it derives the target fire-salt normal bond and places:

- the unbonder on that pair;
- the duplication glyph with fire as its classical source and salt as its
  destination; and
- the prism glyph in one deterministic pose for each black, red and yellow
  channel.

An SHA-256 fingerprint covers every arm, its complete program and every track.
The fingerprint is asserted equal before and after each transplant. Only the
input, unbonder, duplication glyph and prism glyph may move.

Interpretation
--------------
OMSim is authoritative for mechanical survival and completion, but its adapter
does not expose a chemistry event trace. The chemistry evidence comes from the
local diagnostic replay of the exact serialized candidate, while OMSim proves
that the same candidate has no authoritative mechanical failure before the
cycle limit. The result is therefore a strong crossover milestone, not a claim
of puzzle completion.
