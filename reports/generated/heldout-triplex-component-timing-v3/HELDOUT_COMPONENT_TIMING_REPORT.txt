Physical-component timing repair
--------------------------------
The earlier schedule repair shifted whole fragment groups. Several fragments
shared one physical arm and each carried a copy of the same complete donor
program, so a conflict-free group shift usually became a global time offset.
It did not change the machine's mechanical sequence.

The new repair operates on the materialized physical component instead. For a
unique programmable arm it can:

- insert one or two idle cycles before a program suffix;
- remove one or two cycles only where an existing gap permits it;
- reject negative cycles and instruction overlaps before simulation;
- target instruction boundaries near early motion, useful chemistry events,
  the first local error, and existing idle gaps;
- preserve the exact instruction order and all machine geometry.

Replay diagnostics now expose the exact cycle and event kind for required and
general chemistry. This connects the target manufacturing plan directly to
program-cutpoint selection.

Search protocol
---------------
The strict benchmark used the same three held-out assemblies and geometric
budget as the preceding milestone:

- 729 geometric variants per assembly, 2,187 total;
- 96-cycle geometric preflight;
- eight geometric promotions per assembly;
- six progress/survival source geometries per assembly;
- up to 48 physical-component timing edits per source;
- timing radius of two cycles and 16 cutpoints per arm;
- 588 distinct timing variants in total;
- ten concurrent OMSim workers, the configured maximum.

OMSim validates every timing variant, not only variants promoted by the local
simulator. Local replay still supplies chemistry and manipulation evidence,
but it can no longer overrule an OMSim collision, track error, or instruction
conflict.

Oracle-guided global portfolio
------------------------------
Selection previously happened independently inside each assembly family. That
reserved result capacity even for a family with no mechanically valid child.
The new global portfolio reallocates capacity across all families after OMSim
validation while retaining separate progress and survival fronts.

At a 90-result budget:

- preceding balanced geometry portfolio: 23 cycle-limit survivors, 64
  collisions, and three other mechanical errors;
- component timing with local-only final selection: 29 cycle-limit survivors,
  46 collisions, 14 track errors, and one instruction conflict;
- global OMSim-guided portfolio: 90 cycle-limit survivors, zero collisions,
  zero track errors, and zero instruction conflicts.

The final 90 are drawn from the two useful families: 32 from assembly rank 1
and 58 from assembly rank 2. This is a gain of 67 mechanically valid retained
machines over the preceding milestone and eliminates all authoritative
mechanical failures from the returned portfolio.

What the solver can now do
--------------------------
The solver can now:

- mutate the actual program of a shared physical arm rather than fragment
  schedule copies;
- place tempo edits at target-aware chemistry and failure boundaries;
- preserve valid program ordering while inserting or removing idle cycles;
- preflight all variants locally and promote a bounded progress/survival set;
- validate every component-timing variant with OMSim through the standard
  generation command;
- run up to ten concurrent OMSim validations;
- classify cycle-limit, collision, track, instruction-conflict, other, and
  completed outcomes;
- select a global oracle-guided portfolio across assembly families;
- expose component-timing attempts to persistent outcome learning.

It cannot yet:

- produce any product on strict held-out OM2021_W1;
- find target-relevant chemistry in a machine that also survives OMSim;
- repair the track-coordinate divergence in the chemically active third donor
  family;
- combine the stable geometry of one family with the useful chemistry geometry
  of another.

Next decisive milestone
-----------------------
Use the 128 OMSim-stable timing variants as hard mechanical parents, then
search chemistry-part transforms relative to their arm workspace rather than
mutating every fragment join. Crossover should preserve the stable arm,
program, input, and track subgraph while transplanting one required glyph
interaction at a time: bond removal, duplication, prism bonding, then output.
OMSim remains the admission gate after every stage.
