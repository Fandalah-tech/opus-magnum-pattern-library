Why the old score was insufficient
----------------------------------
The previous stage ranked chemistry by event families. A stationary unbonder
and prism could remove and recreate a bond hundreds of times, so a high event
count did not prove that any useful molecule survived.

The new ordered-chemistry analyzer reads the molecular world after every
cycle. A state only counts after it exists in at least two consecutive
snapshots. It tracks the same atom IDs through:

- removal of the original normal bond;
- salt-to-fire duplication;
- accumulation of red, black and yellow on the same atom pair;
- restoration of the transformed fire atom to salt; and
- product delivery, when present.

This converts the ranking target from "an operation fired" to "the intended
chemical result still exists later".
