New generalization and legality controls
----------------------------------------
The solver now understands that one bonder-prisma is physically capable of
all three triplex channels. It may infer an unobserved channel only when a
replay-backed bonding convergence has at least three inputs. Inferred evidence
is recorded separately and receives a ranking penalty.

Fragment geometry is now stored per source solution. A coherent assembly uses
the geometry, transforms, and timing from the same donor instead of creating a
chimera from unrelated representatives.

Target part availability is checked before local simulation. Optional donor
parts disabled by the target are removed and reported. For this benchmark the
removed parts were one unification glyph or one Van Berlo arm, depending on
the donor. The required unbonder, duplication glyph, prism, input, output, and
remaining legal arm/track parts were retained.

Bounded repair experiment
-------------------------
The geometric search can now add unobserved local hex translations and small
rotations around engine-observed joins. A two-stage evaluator replays every
variant for 96 cycles, then promotes only eight survivors per assembly to a
full replay. This prevents thousands of fruitless 6,000-cycle simulations.

Assemblies searched: 3
Geometric variants evaluated: 2,187 (729 per assembly)
Variants promoted to full replay: 24
Products delivered: 0
Complete variants: 0

The repair data exposed the current structural bottleneck. Several canonical
fragments share the same physical donor parts. Perturbing one fragment join
therefore duplicates overlapping parts, growing a nine-part baseline to
11-15 parts, instead of moving only the intended input, arm, or glyph. More
search budget cannot fix that representation error.

Current capability level
------------------------
The solver can now:

- enforce a strict leave-one-puzzle-out learning boundary;
- prove that learned evidence contains no target source;
- combine cross-puzzle chemistry using a guarded physical inference;
- retain source-specific geometry and timing provenance;
- remove and report target-disabled optional components;
- generate legal, round-trip-clean candidate solution files;
- route collision and inactivity failures to bounded repair;
- preflight thousands of variants before expensive full replay;
- preserve every rejection as structured learning evidence.

It cannot yet:

- decompose shared donor machinery into independently movable components;
- graft the missing triplex behaviour without duplicating shared parts;
- deliver a product on the strict OM2021_W1 held-out benchmark.

Next decisive milestone
-----------------------
Replace fragment-level placement with a shared-component graph. Each physical
part will have one identity even when several functional fragments reference
it. Repairs will then move or replace the responsible component and propagate
that change through every dependent fragment. The same 2,187-outcome corpus
can be reused to prioritize the input/arm subgraph instead of expanding the
global search blindly.
