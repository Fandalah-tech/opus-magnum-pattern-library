Why the architecture changed
----------------------------
The earlier planner described this chemistry as a linear six-step chain:

  remove bond -> duplicate -> red/black/yellow triplex bonds -> deliver

Materialization and replay showed that this was chemically complete but
mechanically incomplete. The puzzle starts from one reagent, yet duplication
creates several flows that must reconverge. The solver now derives convergence
arity from multi-input manufacturing operations, selects a same-solution
convergence motif, and keeps every branch and tail transition coherent with
that source solution.

Geometry correction learned during replay
------------------------------------------
The .solution format stores track cells as offsets relative to the track
origin. Canonicalization and fragment transplantation previously treated those
offsets as absolute world cells. The generated rail could therefore move far
away while retaining a misleadingly identical canonical hash.

The correction now:
  - rotates track offsets without translating them;
  - reconstructs absolute rail footprints only for diagnostics/simulation;
  - keeps conduit cells in their part-local coordinate frame;
  - rebuilds every fragment identity and flow transition after the fix.

Honest boundary
