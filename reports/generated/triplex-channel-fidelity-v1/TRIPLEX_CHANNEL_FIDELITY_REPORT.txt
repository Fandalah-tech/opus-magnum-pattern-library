TRIPLEX CHANNEL FIDELITY REPORT
===============================

Status: VALIDATED

Scope
-----
Preserve the exact red, black and yellow triplex bond channels from puzzle
parsing through molecule fingerprints, cross-puzzle retrieval, blind transfer,
input spawning, prismatic bonding and output validation.

Implementation
--------------
- Binary bond codes 2, 4 and 8 retain red, black and yellow respectively.
- Binary bond code 14 retains the combined red+black+yellow identity.
- Molecule hashes remain translation- and rotation-invariant while treating
  different triplex channel masks as different chemistry.
- Feature index schema 0.2.0 exposes coarse bond counts and exact bond variants.
- The prismatic bonder creates black, red and yellow bonds on its three exact
  local edges and can accumulate different channels on the same atom pair.
- Input and output models expand a combined mask into three engine bond kinds.
- A normal bonder no longer creates a normal bond over an existing triplex bond.
