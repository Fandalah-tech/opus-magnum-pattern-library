Important correction to milestone v5
-------------------------------------
The preceding report called 22 intermediate candidates "OMSim-stable
calcified complete-triplex" candidates. That wording was too strong.

OMSim's ordinary cycle-limit validation proved mechanical survival, but did not
inspect the intermediate molecule's chemistry. A new product-metric test then
exposed a simulator fidelity error: the local engine processed unbonders and
prisms in separate type buckets, while OMSim processes instantaneous glyphs in
solution-file order. When both touch the same pair during one half-cycle, that
ordering changes the resulting bond.

Those 22 historical results are therefore reclassified as cycle-limit
survivors, not authoritative chemistry proofs. The current milestone does not
depend on that claim.

Engine fidelity repair
----------------------
The runtime and faithful simulators now retain the solution-file order of the
entire bonder family:

- normal bonder;
- unbonder;
- prismatic bonder; and
- multi-bonder.

A regression test covers the critical case. Prism-then-unbonder leaves no
bond; unbonder-then-prism leaves the expected triplex channel. After the fix,
the local atom positions and bonds matched the instrumented OMSim trace across
the decisive cycles. The temporary trace instrumentation was removed before
verification.

Automatic product-completion stage
----------------------------------
The new solver stage performs the following operations without target solution
examples:

1. Stably orders unbonders before prisms when that chemistry order is needed.
2. Detects a persistent fire-fire-salt core with two complete triplex edges.
3. Identifies the fire endpoint that must receive the fourth atom.
4. Rotates and translates a bounded supply/finisher template around that
   observed endpoint.
5. Adds an isolated fire supply, an unbonder, two prisms and two short arm
   programs.
6. Derives the output pose from the finished molecule.
7. Replays locally, then promotes only delivered products to OMSim's explicit
   product metric.

The template is invariant under all six hex rotations. It schedules the supply
arm relative to the observed core-ready cycle rather than using a fixed global
timestamp.

What the solver can now do
--------------------------
The solver can now automatically turn a persistent three-atom triplex core
into one exact held-out product, place it on the output, and distinguish an
official product proof from mere collision-free survival. This entire stage is
available through the composed-candidate pipeline and its CLI.

It still cannot reset and repeat the mechanism safely. The remaining failure is
now a repeatability/cleanup problem rather than an unknown-product-chemistry
problem: stale atoms and the original mechanism eventually collide on cycle
212 after the first accepted output.

Next decisive milestone
-----------------------
Convert the one-shot arm programs into a repeatable period, evacuate or reuse
the atoms left by each reagent spawn, and keep the core mechanism clear through
six accepted deliveries. OMSim's product metric can then be raised from one to
six, giving an unambiguous full-puzzle proof.
