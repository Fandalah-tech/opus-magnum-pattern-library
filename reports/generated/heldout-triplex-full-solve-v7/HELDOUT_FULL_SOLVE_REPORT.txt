From one-shot output to repeatable solve
----------------------------------------
The preceding milestone delivered one exact product at cycle 61 but left the
machine running through a long donor program. That program created additional
partially processed reagents, accumulated them on the board, and collided on
cycle 212.

The new stage identifies the exact prefix that made the first persistent
three-atom core. It appends a physical reset immediately after the core is
released. OMSim's reset expansion returns the piston, rotation and track
position to their initial state, producing a genuine 59-cycle chemistry block.

The fourth-fire supply initially seemed to require one reagent per product.
Instead, the solver now uses the entire auxiliary reagent:

- the first unbonder isolates its first fire atom;
- a second unbonder separates the remaining fire-salt pair;
- duplication converts that salt into a third fire atom; and
- one tracked feeder consumes those three isolated fires over three consecutive
  chemistry blocks.

After the third fire leaves the input footprint, the auxiliary input respawns.
The three-block supply schedule therefore forms a 177-cycle super-period. Two
super-periods provide exactly the six fourth-fire atoms required by the puzzle.

Automatic solver stage
----------------------
The repeating-product stage is integrated after the authoritative one-product
stage. It now:

1. finds the arm that produced the accepted three-atom core;
2. truncates the irrelevant donor tail;
3. measures the physical reset-expanded block period;
4. unrolls three core-production blocks;
5. adds the second unbonder, duplication glyph and three-cell feeder track;
6. schedules the feeder across all three auxiliary atoms;
7. repeats the final pivot/output operation for each core;
8. locally requires at least six product-delivery events; and
9. promotes only those candidates to OMSim's `product 6 cycles` oracle.

The geometry is translated and rotated from the detected product-core endpoint,
including the feeder track offsets. The oracle worker count remains capped at
ten.

What this milestone establishes
-------------------------------
This is no longer an intermediate chemistry score or a collision-free timeout.
OMSim accepts the normal solution contract after all six products. The held-out
pipeline has crossed the complete sequence:

- donor-only retrieval;
- cross-puzzle mechanical composition;
- timing and geometry repair;
- active chemistry transplantation;
- persistent exact-channel triplex construction;
- exact fourth-atom attachment and output placement; and
- reset-safe repeated manufacture.

The solver is not yet a universal Opus Magnum solver. The final repeating
template currently targets this family of a three-atom core plus an auxiliary
three-atom fire supply. The next development frontier is to infer comparable
reset and resource-reuse schedules for other product topologies rather than
encoding a separate finisher for each one.
